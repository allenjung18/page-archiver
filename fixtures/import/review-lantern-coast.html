Review: The Lantern Coast

By Reuben Ashe

The Lantern Coast is a slow novel about a fast-disappearing village, and it earns its slowness. Each chapter follows a different house through a single winter.

The author's invented dialect, with words like glimmerhaw for the light before a storm, never feels like decoration; it is how the villagers keep their weather.

If the ending is too tidy, the middle is wonderful. Read it in winter.
