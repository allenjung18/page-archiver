Notes from a Night Train

By Sol Amari

The sleeper left at ten. By midnight the corridor had become a street: people in socks, a card game, a conductor selling tea from a dented urn.

At the border the train stopped for an hour in a station lit like an aquarium. Somebody called the stillness the drowsenhalt, and the word stuck with the whole carriage.

We woke in a different country with the same view of the same fields, which is the whole charm of it.
