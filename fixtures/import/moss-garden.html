Starting a Moss Garden in a Shady Corner

By Ines Varga

Moss wants shade, acid soil and steady moisture, and very little else. Scrape the corner bare, firm it down, and press small patches into the surface like tiles.

Water with rainwater for the first month; tap water can carry enough lime to stall it. Our neighbour swears by a mist of buttermilk, which she calls the frondlemist method, though the moss seems indifferent.

By autumn the gaps close. Walk on it gently and it forgives you.
