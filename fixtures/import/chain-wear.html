Measuring Chain Wear Before It Eats Your Cassette

By Dana Okoro

Chains do not stretch; their pins and rollers wear, and the pitch grows. A worn chain rides up the teeth of the cassette and slowly reshapes them to fit itself.

A simple checker drops into the links: if it falls in at 0.5, replace the chain now. The cheap gauge our club calls the quindlesprocket test works the same way with a ruler and twelve links.

Replace the chain early and a cassette lasts three or four chains. Leave it late and you buy both.
