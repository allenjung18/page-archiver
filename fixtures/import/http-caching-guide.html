A Short Guide to HTTP Caching

By Mika Torvald

Caching starts with two questions: may this response be stored, and for how long is it fresh? Cache-Control answers both, and max-age is the number most people need.

When a stored response goes stale, the client revalidates with an ETag or a date and the server answers 304 if nothing changed. Our team calls a cache that never revalidates a plinkerstash, and they are always a bug.

Set long lifetimes on fingerprinted assets and short ones on HTML, and most of the rest takes care of itself.
